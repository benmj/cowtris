Cowtris 2.1 UI and help strings
===============================

Status: confirmed embedded strings, except where noted.

Options menu
------------

Use Special &Cows

The ampersand marks the keyboard mnemonic in a VB menu caption. The p-code selection
helper also reads a Boolean gate before it permits breeds 8 through 10. The original
variable and event-handler names are not retained and are therefore inferred in the
VB-style reconstruction.

Instructions
------------

Mad Cow, Holy cow, and Purple Cow:
From time to time, these special one-block cows will appear
and perform unique tasks:
Mad Cow: Destroys all blocks touching it when it lands
Holy Cow: Falls through other blocks, destroying them
Purple Cow: Removes the row it lands in, even if it isn't 
complete

Notes
-----

- Capitalization and line breaks above follow the executable's embedded strings.
- "Touching" is implemented as the eight surrounding cells of the Mad Cow's 3x3
  neighborhood; the center cell containing the Mad Cow is cleared as well.
- The modern port intentionally phrases the Holy Cow description as “tunnels a hole
  through blocks” to make the hole/holy pun explicit.
